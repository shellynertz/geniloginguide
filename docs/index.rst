Steps to Access Geni Login Account
----------------------------------

Logging into your Geni account is simple when you follow the correct steps.

Visit the Official Geni Website
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Open your preferred web browser on your computer, tablet, or smartphone. Enter the official Geni website address carefully in the browser bar. Verify that you are on the legitimate website before entering your login credentials to avoid phishing attempts.

Locate the Sign In Option
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

On the homepage, find the login or sign in button, usually located at the top right corner of the page. Click this option to access the Geni login page.

Enter Your Registered Email Address
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

On the login screen, type the email address associated with your Geni account. Double-check for typos or extra spaces, as incorrect details can prevent successful login.

Enter Your Password
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Carefully type your password into the password field. Passwords are case sensitive, so ensure uppercase and lowercase letters are entered accurately. A secure password manager can autofill credentials safely for convenience.

Access Your Geni Dashboard
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

After entering your login credentials, click the sign in button. If your information is correct, you will be directed to your Geni dashboard. From here, you can manage your family tree, explore ancestry records, and interact with relatives and collaborators.

Using the Geni Mobile App
-------------------------

Geni also offers a mobile app for convenient access to your family tree and genealogy tools. To log in through the app:

- Download the Geni app from your device’s app store  
- Open the app and select sign in  
- Enter your registered email and password  
- Tap the login option to access your dashboard  

The mobile app allows you to update family trees, attach records, and communicate with relatives while on the move.

How to Recover a Forgotten Password
-----------------------------------

If you forget your Geni login password, recovery is simple.

Use the Password Reset Option
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

On the login page, select the forgot password link. Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions sent to your inbox. The password reset email will guide you in creating a new secure password.

Create a Strong Password
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

When setting a new password, use a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid simple or predictable passwords. A strong password protects your genealogical data and personal information from unauthorized access.

Common Geni Login Issues and Solutions
--------------------------------------

Users may occasionally face difficulties logging into Geni. Below are common issues and ways to resolve them.

Incorrect Email or Password
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Verify that your login credentials are entered correctly. Even small typing mistakes can prevent successful login.

Browser Cache and Cookies
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Clearing your browser cache and cookies can resolve login problems caused by stored data. After clearing, refresh the page and attempt to log in again.

Internet Connectivity Problems
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A weak or unstable internet connection may prevent the login page from loading or processing credentials. Ensure your internet connection is stable before trying again.

Account Lock or Security Measures
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Multiple unsuccessful login attempts may temporarily restrict access for security purposes. Wait briefly before retrying or reset your password if necessary.

Contact Geni Support
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If troubleshooting steps do not resolve login issues, contact Geni customer support. Provide your registered email address and a detailed description of the problem for assistance.
